## Engineering principles

**1. Production first.** Latency, cost, failure modes, and observability beat demo polish.

**2. Composable structure.** Keep retrieval/generation, session/tools, and control/data planes separable.

**3. Observability from day one.** You cannot improve what you cannot see.

**4. Measure, then iterate.** Baselines before prompt, chunking, retrieval, or model changes.
